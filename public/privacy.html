Keyflame privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Keyflame is a typing game for children. We built it so that it does not need, ask for, or collect personal information from anyone.

What stays on your device

Progress (rider names, dragons, lesson stars, race results and settings) is saved in your browser's own storage on your device. It is not sent to us. Rider and dragon names are picked from preset word lists, so children never type their real names. Clearing your browser data deletes this progress unless you have made a backup code.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos or locations.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No chat or messaging between players.
  • No tracking cookies or advertising identifiers set by Keyflame.

Advertising

Keyflame may show a small number of ads on menu and results screens to pay for hosting. Ads never appear during typing. Because the site is directed to children, ads are limited to non-personalized, contextual ads: they are chosen based on the page, not on information about the viewer. The ad provider (Google AdSense) may process technical data such as the IP address to deliver and protect ads, under its own policies for child-directed sites. Families who buy the supporter pack see no ads.

Supporter pack

The optional supporter pack is bought by a parent or guardian through a payment page run by our payment provider. We never see or store card details. A grown-up check is shown before the payment page opens.

Fonts and hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive standard technical information (like the IP address and browser type) needed to deliver files.

Parents' rights

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing stored with us to review or delete. To remove all Keyflame data from a device, use "Remove rider" in Settings or clear the browser's site data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/davidbalasa-ui/keyflame/issues.
