JOINED: yes, first try. Typed SLF ZQJ / 1 / ROBIN on "🐉 Join a wing" and it showed "🐉 My wing" with code SLF ZQJ, #1, ROBIN.

DID:
- Lessons passed: 1 (F and J, 92% correct, 1 star, +10 embers). Typing took 134s with 5 slips.
- Class race: 1 finished ("1st Place!", 5 WPM, 86% accuracy). Reached it through Race > Wing race > I'm ready.
- Solo races (Volcano Canyon, Cinderfang): 4 finished (2nd, 2nd, 4th, 3rd). Egg warmth went 42% > 60% > 79% > 97% > ready.
- Eggs hatched: 1 (Emberback egg, came out as "Striped Cinder" after 5 taps).

STUCK OR CONFUSED:
- Home screen "Join a wing" button is small text, but it is the only bright thing with a dragon icon, so found it. OK.
- After tapping "I'm ready!", the screen said "Not ready / Waiting for a classmate to be ready..." with no timer and no sign of a countdown. Ava did not know if anything was happening. Only nothing-to-do; she waited 20 seconds and then the race started on its own.
- The class race screen has a "Not ready" button that does nothing useful to a kid who is ready. Confusing wording.
- The bottom keyboard in the typing screen rearranged between races (";" moved from the right pinky area, the bottom row changed order). Ava noticed the keys looked different and did not understand why.
- In the typing screens, the race ended with letters still left untyped in two races (9 letters left, then 11 letters left, "the screen stopped taking keys"). The result pop-up appeared anyway with a normal placing. Ava would not notice, but it looks like the race timed out or ended early.
- Lessons menu: I accidentally opened Lessons (tapping by an old number after the screen changed). Ava would also just tap around. Harmless, but the numbered buttons shift between screens.
- Wording: "Daily flight", "Beat rival dragons", "Emberback egg", "Warmth", "Embers" are not words a 9 year old reads quickly. Ava skipped most of the text and went by the big colored buttons.

BROKEN:
- Race ended with letters still untyped (9 and 11 letters left) and the screen stopped taking keys. Possible real bug: race end by timer or a key-handling issue. Worth checking.
- "Not ready" label on the class race is unclear when already ready; no countdown while waiting for classmates.
- Keyboard layout on the typing screen changed between races (rows reordered).

FUN: Yes, Ava would keep playing. The egg warming up and the hatch with the big tap-the-egg count is very fun for a button-masher, and the first race win felt great. The lessons were slow and the waiting after "I'm ready" was boring, but the egg reward kept her going.
