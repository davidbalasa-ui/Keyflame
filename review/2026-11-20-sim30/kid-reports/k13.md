DID:
- Lessons passed: 1 ("F and J", 2 stars, 97% correct, 7 WPM). Daily flight then showed "Done! See you tomorrow".

STUCK OR CONFUSED:
- Home tile "🐉 Join a wing" reads like a question for a 10-year-old: I did not know a "wing" was my class until I was in it. The teacher said "Join our class", so I had to think about it.
- Class race screen: after tapping "🏁 I'm ready!" the button turns into "Not ready" with "Waiting for a classmate to be ready…". Milo does not know if he is racing yet or if he has to wait for everyone. Had to wait 20s, then just started typing when the race screen showed up.
- Class board showed names like "Sunny Comet 16 WPM" with icons ⌨️ / 📱 next to them. Milo wondered what the phone and keyboard icons mean (keyboard vs iPad?). No label.
- Class race screen: after the race, the page with "Here now" and "Racing" labels showed others as "Racing" even after the race had ended. Confusing.
- Nest screen: "110 keys to hatch" and "Race to hatch" plus "🔥 Use 100 embers / Need 61 more 🔥". Milo did not know whether 110 keys means a lot of typing or what hatching needs. Choosing "Race to hatch" (because the teacher said so) worked.
- The "Get an egg" card with "🔥 150 / Need 89 more" appears right after hatching. Milo wants a new egg right away and does not see how to start one except by earning embers.
- Scrolled-off items on Nest: "Your aerie" rewards (Moss rug 🔥40 ... Starry ceiling 🔥250) are far below. Milo collects things, so he wants to see them, but nothing said "shop".
- Lesson screen: "Next: 'f' with your left thumb" is a lot for a 10-year-old on an iPad. He did not know how the 3 stars were earned until the result screen said "10 WPM at 95% = third star".

BROKEN:
- Class race finish screen said "Great flying! You finished 4th of 4" with a popup, but the class board list order did not match the race result list (board showed Gentle Flyer at 6 WPM, result said 5 WPM). Looks like a number mismatch.
- Race result "Egg warmth" went 42% -> 63% -> 87%, but the egg appeared to need "110 keys to hatch" on the Nest screen; hatch then happened at 100% with no clear message about the threshold.
- Hatch: after "Hatch your egg", the screen said "Tap to open!" and "5 more taps". Fine, but "5 more taps" on a tap-to-open egg was not explained.
- The rename popup "Meet Zephyrscale!" appeared on its own after hatching. Milo did not choose the name; it was pre-filled. Not broken, just surprising.

FUN: Yes, Milo would keep playing. Hatching his own dragon and getting a new name and a new title was the best part, and he wants to collect more eggs and see the aerie rewards. Racing against classmates was OK but the waiting and the "Not ready" button made him feel stuck and then he got 4th place, which stung a bit.

COMMANDS USED: about 30 (including note and tap calls, not counting this write).
