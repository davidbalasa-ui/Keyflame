# Keyflame full UX review

Reviewed the post-#66 build represented by the captures and corresponding source. The pass covers 44 states each at phone (412 × 740), iPad upright (810 × 1080), iPad sideways (1080 × 810), laptop (1366 × 768), and desktop (1920 × 1080), plus 21 classroom captures. Classroom sizes below are viewport sizes. This is a visual and code review, with interaction checks to run after fixes.

## Ranked findings

1. **Race results can cover rewards.** On 412 × 740, `phone/09-race-results-home-nest-race-again.jpg` and `phone/27-race-results-faster-than-usual.jpg` show the Courses/Home/Nest/Race again controls over the final reward row. The same overlap is visible at 1080 × 810 in `ipad-sideways/27-race-results-faster-than-usual.jpg`. The 1920 × 1080 version has room. The celebration is scrollable and the action bar sticks inside it, but the initial view gives a child little reason to look under the bar. On short viewports give the rewards their own scroll area above a separate footer and keep Home, Nest, and Race again visible. Check a six-reward finish in phone Safari and iPad landscape. The last tile and its caption should scroll fully into view above the buttons.

2. **Make the first lesson and the first race obvious.** At 412 × 740 and 810 × 1080, `phone/02-lessons-new-rider.jpg` and `ipad-upright/02-lessons-new-rider.jpg` give the optional typing check the brightest button, while F and J looks much like the locked lessons. At 412 × 740, `phone/07-race-map-with-tab-bar.jpg` puts a large Wing race banner above the first solo course, Volcano Canyon, even for an unjoined rider. For a 7–9-year-old, put an illustrated “Start F and J” button above the lesson grid and a visible play button on Volcano Canyon. Keep “Already know some typing?” with the optional check as a secondary action for returning or older kids. Make Wing race secondary until they join a wing.

3. **Help a class join and hand off shared iPads.** At 412 × 860, `classroom/2b-phone-join.jpg` makes the three-field form the obvious route and puts “Or scan your card with the camera” in fine print. At 820 × 1180, `classroom/9-ipad-wrong-word.jpg` names the wrong word and badge in the error message, but the child still has to find the input to correct. `classroom/9c-ipad-qr-joined.jpg` shows the joined rider, but the next classmate has no obvious “Change rider” action. Put a camera illustration and “Scan your badge with the Camera app” above the manual option, highlight and focus the field that needs fixing, and add “Change rider” beside the joined nickname and badge. Confirm the next rider before going back to the lobby. Put the transfer QR behind a separate “Move to another device” action.

4. **Distinguish ready, waiting, and starting in a class race.** In `classroom/3-phone-lobby.jpg` and `classroom/4-phone-starting-soon.jpg` at 412 × 860, a child can tap “Race!” and still be waiting, then sees “Starting soon…” without a cue about what to do. At 1280 × 800 in `classroom/6-teacher-watching.jpg` and `classroom/8-teacher-board.jpg`, the illustration takes the space a teacher needs for rider status. Label the pre-start action “Ready,” show “Waiting for your class” while waiting, and show a countdown when scheduled. Put the ready/finished counts and rider list above the illustration.

5. **Fix the teacher progress list at phone width.** At 412 × 860 in `classroom/8d-teacher-progress-phone.jpg`, “Last active” wraps onto its own header row, while each student mixes speed, accuracy, lessons, and date below. It is hard to tell which figure belongs to which heading. Give each phone row explicit metric labels, keep the name and badge together, and put secondary actions such as CSV export in an overflow menu. The tablet grid in `classroom/8c-teacher-progress-ipad.jpg` (820 × 1180) already has room for the table.

6. **Name the next action on Home and in the Nest.** At 412 × 740, `phone/28-home-egg-ready-daily-go.jpg` offers “Go” for Daily flight, but `phone/30-word-challenge-from-daily-go.jpg` shows that it opens Animals in this state. The hub source also routes this button to a lesson or the race map in other states. Label the button with the actual destination (“Start lesson,” “Play Animals,” or “Choose a race”). In `phone/05-nest-from-results.jpg`, “245 keys to hatch” and “Race to warm it” ask a child to connect two terms for the same goal. Use “Race to hatch” and keep the key count. On `phone/33-hatch-tapping.jpg`, put the number of taps left beside the existing dots. Home and Nest already have recognizable icons and consistent finish-screen actions in `phone/04-lesson-results-home-nest-next.jpg` and `phone/31-word-challenge-results.jpg`. The separate Home/results shortcut in pending PR #62 has its own review in #63.

7. **Put the two classroom entry points first.** At 412 × 740 in `phone/27g-grown-ups-classroom-start.jpg` and 1366 × 768 in `pc-laptop-1366/27g-grown-ups-classroom-start.jpg`, the hero and setup instructions come before the forms. A teacher trying to reopen a class has to scroll past the new-class path. Put “Create class” and “Open existing class” immediately under the heading and show the relevant form when chosen. Keep the print actions visible once the class is open, as in `classroom/1-teacher-ready.jpg` (1280 × 800).

8. **Make the grown-up privacy summary match the policy.** At 412 × 740, `phone/19-grown-ups.jpg` says “No ads, no tracking” and “Progress stays on this device.” `phone/27h-privacy-page.jpg` explains classroom activity, optional reports, and anonymous daily totals. Use “No ads or ad tracking” and “Most progress stays on this device” on the hub, with a short link to the exceptions. `phone/27i-terms-page.jpg` already states who can play and that the supporter pack is optional. Both policy pages have a return link at the bottom in source. Add a small “Back to Keyflame” link near the top so it is easy to return after following the privacy link.

9. **Give Family, Ideas, and Support one clear job each.** At 412 × 740, `phone/27d-grown-ups-family.jpg` jumps from compact stats to a WPM chart. Put “On this device” and one meaningful next practice action near the rider name so a parent can use the screen without interpreting a trend. In `phone/27e-grown-ups-ideas.jpg`, keep the data warning beside the text box, with “Ask a grown-up to send” visible near the form and a short explanation of the check. In `phone/27f-grown-ups-support.jpg` and `ipad-upright/27f-grown-ups-support.jpg` (810 × 1080), the long pitch precedes the “coming soon” status and the existing code form. Put “Supporter pack coming soon” and “Redeem a code” together near the top, then list the optional pack benefits.

10. **Taste: let the hatch reveal finish before asking for name choices.** At 412 × 740, `phone/34-hatch-reveal.jpg` makes the dragon the reward, while `phone/35-hatch-name-card.jpg` quickly adds arrows, reroll, and links. Keep the reveal and default name, put “Done” first, and offer “Change name” as a single secondary action. This keeps a readable exit for a younger player and preserves customization for older kids.

`phone/24-lessons-tricky-keys-button.jpg` through `phone/26-tricky-keys-results.jpg` (412 × 740) already form a clear practice loop for ages 10–13. The entry and results name the same focus keys and the unrelated mastery reward is explicitly marked “Bonus.” Preserve that distinction in the race-results pass.
