# Keyflame navigation + learning review — 18 November 2026

**Sources:** Screenshot branch `claude/project-thread-nrtu77`: all 32 phone screens at 412×740; classroom teacher/phone flows (0, 1, 2b, 2c, 3–8); representative iPad portrait/landscape and laptop screens (race 08, results 09/23, warm-up 22, tricky drill 25). Read `src/ui/finishBar.ts`, `src/ui/tabBar.ts`, `src/ui/screens/{hub,lessons,race,classroom}.ts`, `src/game/improve.ts`, and `src/content/challenges.ts`. Screenshot bot's ~80 WPM is deliberately **not** a finding.

## Verdict

The PR #51 dead-end fix is effective: a seven-year-old can now find Home and Nest from the new main tab bar and from lesson/race/check results; the giant orange Next / Again buttons are clear. The tapping finger on the new-rider egg and explicit `Nest · %` in `phone/01-home-new-rider-nest-finger.jpg`, the lack of finger after opening in `phone/06-home-after-nest-seen.jpg`, and the ready-egg treatment in `phone/28-home-egg-ready-daily-go.jpg` are good. Keep these. This is a meaningful usability improvement, but there are several important phone-specific defects/teaching ambiguities to fix before calling it instruction-free.

## Ranked findings

1. **P0 — Full touch keyboard is off-screen during solo race and Tricky Keys on the reference phone.** In `phone/08-race-running.jpg`, only the first two keyboard rows and the beginning of the third are visible; the spacebar is completely outside the captured 412×740 viewport. `phone/25-tricky-keys-drill.jpg` similarly cuts off the bottom of the third keyboard row and the spacebar. The same touch keyboard fits in `phone/03-first-lesson-hands-picture.jpg`, `phone/22-warm-up-lesson.jpg`, `phone/30-word-challenge-from-daily-go.jpg` and `classroom/5-phone-racing.jpg`. A child playing solo race or Tricky Keys cannot reliably tap every required key without scrolling, even though the dragon race continues. **Fix:** use one shared touch-playing layout with the keyboard pinned entirely within the visual viewport (including spacebar and bottom safe area); reduce track height, header chrome, and explanatory margins before shrinking keys below 44px. Prefer dynamic `dvh`/safe-area-aware sizing and move decorative race art upward. **Acceptance:** on a 412×740 phone and a shorter 360×640 phone, all keys/space are visible and tapable simultaneously in solo race, classroom race, lessons, tricky drills and word challenges, with no scrolling during active input. iPad portrait and landscape race 08 show the complete board; this is a phone-specific regression.

2. **P0 — The race finish dock obscures real earned rewards.** `phone/09-race-results-home-nest-race-again.jpg` puts four huge reward tiles below the medal and speed gauge, with a fifth achievement card buried under the sticky `Courses / Home / Nest / Race again` dock. `phone/27-race-results-faster-than-usual.jpg` covers a card headed `Duneskimmer egg` and other reward text; the bottom reward is not fully readable even though it matters emotionally. `pc-laptop-1366/09-race-results-home-nest-race-again.jpg` looks much cleaner and confirms the mobile-specific overflow. **Fix:** collapse the first glance to trophy + speed/accuracy + 1 compact reward row (`+45 embers · egg +57 · New egg!`); put all other rewards behind `See rewards (N)` or in a naturally scrollable list with measured padding *above* a nonoverlapping sticky action dock. Keep Home, Nest and orange Race again always present. Test maximal rewards (new egg + several mastered keys + achievements) and large text. Don't make the child drag-scroll to find the first reward.

3. **P1 — Touch-thumb coaching is inconsistent with key location and desktop finger coaching.** `phone/22-warm-up-lesson.jpg` explicitly says `Next: “g” with your right thumb`, although G sits just left of the center of the displayed QWERTY keyboard. `phone/25-tricky-keys-drill.jpg` says `Next: “s” with your left ring finger` despite a touch keyboard filling the screen. `phone/10-typing-check.jpg` instructs `“T” with your left pointer finger (hold Shift with the other hand)` even while showing touch keyboard. On a physical keyboard, using the left index for G is standard; on phone touch-screen, a right thumb *might* reach G but the visual conflict confuses a young learner. **Fix:** for touch input say `Tap the glowing G` (and illustrate only the highlighted key), or assign thumbs by actual touch layout; reserve explicit finger names, home-row bumps and Shift instructions for detected physical keyboards. If the app purposely allows typing via a connected keyboard while the virtual board is visible, show `Keyboard` vs `Touch` mode explicitly. Add unit tests for G/F/S/T coaching by input mode.

4. **P1 — Student `Race!` versus teacher `Start a race for everyone` is functionally valid but unclear.** `classroom/3-phone-lobby.jpg` gives a kid a huge `Race!` button; `classroom/4-phone-starting-soon.jpg` changes it to `Cancel` and says `Starting soon…`. In code, pressing it enters waiting and the longest-waiting ready kid can initiate a small voluntary race once two are waiting. Meanwhile `classroom/6-teacher-watching.jpg` correctly gives *only the teacher* `Start a race for everyone`, disabled while the race is in progress. A teacher could reasonably believe the students' Race button initiates a class-wide race, and a waiting child may expect to race immediately. **Fix:** rename kid lobby button to `I'm ready to race`; then `✓ Ready · Leave queue` / `Waiting for 1 more racer` / `Starting in 3…` with a simple countdown. On the teacher panel label `Start race for whole class` and state `Students can also join small races themselves`, if that is intentional. Keep separate teacher-only class-wide start; do not remove kids' voluntary races. Validate disabled/enabled/teacher-start state with 1, 2 and 30 connected students.

5. **P1 — Tricky Keys teaches well once found, but Progress buries the action below charts.** `phone/15-progress-tricky-keys.jpg` presents six numeric summary cards, achievements, then a speed chart; `phone/16-progress-key-map.jpg` shows the attractive target-icon Tricky Keys button only after scrolling to the key map. In contrast `phone/24-lessons-tricky-keys-button.jpg` makes it unmistakable at the very top. A seven-year-old may never discover the Progress shortcut. **Fix:** keep the excellent top-of-Lessons card and add a compact `Practice my tricky keys` shortcut next to the Progress heading or the top stats; reuse the SAME practice screen, no extra dashboard or duplicate mechanics. When there are no weak keys, hide the shortcut as `trickyButton()` already does.

6. **P1 — Tricky Keys reward feedback contradicts the stated practice target.** `phone/25-tricky-keys-drill.jpg` clearly targets **K, D, L**. Then `phone/26-tricky-keys-results.jpg` shows 100% for K/D/L, but awards `J key mastered` and `S key mastered`; in code `payImprovements` checks globally newly-mastered keys rather than only the selected weak set. This may be statistically correct but *feels arbitrary* to a kid who practised K/D/L. **Fix:** distinguish `Today's practice: K, D, L` from `Bonus — you also mastered J and S!`, or defer global-mastery announcements to the Progress summary. Do not punish practice: keep its egg warmth, low stakes and no speed-pressure. In `phone/23-lesson-results-improvement-rewards.jpg`, `Faster than usual!` and `A/F key mastered` are welcome but should include an accessible micro-explanation: `Faster than your last few tries` / `You hit F accurately many times` instead of unexplained labels.

7. **P2 — Blue warm-up rings need a quick, kid-readable meaning.** `phone/22-warm-up-lesson.jpg` labels `Warm-up first` and switches the first rings blue, but a new rider won't know whether blue means a special reward, a penalty or a new lesson. The lesson code constructs warm-up content from earlier learned keys before the main lesson. **Fix:** at first occurrence show a one-time nonblocking, icon-led `🔵 Warm up with keys you already know → 🟡 Learn new keys` hint; colored rings should also have shape/position or a short label for color-blind readers. Once taught, let the rings do the explaining.

8. **P2 — Word challenges and the Daily Go action need clearer forward context after graduation.** `phone/28-home-egg-ready-daily-go.jpg` still says `Daily flight: 0/3 races or 1 lesson` and `Go`, but that button now opens `phone/30-word-challenge-from-daily-go.jpg` (`Animals · Short words`), not a lesson. This is sensible mechanically, but feels unexpected to a child who finished all 20 lessons. `phone/29-lessons-word-challenges.jpg` offers six themed packs and level locks, good progression but long scroll below the completed lessons. **Fix:** when lessons are complete, change the daily subtext to `1 word challenge or 3 races`; show `Next: Animals · Short words` immediately beside Go, and provide a visible `Word challenges` heading at the TOP of Lessons with jump-to-pack or tabs. `phone/31-word-challenge-results.jpg` correctly shows `Next challenge`; keep. `phone/32-nest-egg-ready.jpg` correctly shows `Tap to open!`; keep.

9. **P2 — Preserve visible controls without covering important bottom content on smaller layouts.** `phone/05-nest-from-results.jpg` hides the lower `Next egg…` line under the tab bar; `phone/14-codex.jpg`, `phone/17-achievements.jpg`, and `phone/29-lessons-word-challenges.jpg` all show scrolling content extending behind the persistent bar. This may be navigable by scrolling, but test the final item's visibility and keyboard focus, including landscape and larger text. `ipad-sideways/23-lesson-results-improvement-rewards.jpg` shows the result modal within the available viewport; desktop and tablet designs should remain relatively unchanged. **Fix:** apply tab-bar height + safe-area `padding-bottom` to all scroll containers, not ad-hoc spacers. Test focus-scroll and last-card reachability before calling it complete.

10. **P2 — Reduce accidental race-abandon/navigation ambiguity during timed input.** `phone/08-race-running.jpg` has `← Courses` at the top; the new destination naming is better than a generic Back, but an accidental tap during a race may abandon the session without warning. If that behavior actually discards progress, display a short `Leave race? Progress won't count` confirmation (not for already-finished screens). Do not block normal Home/Nest exit paths on results. Consider this an interaction-to-verify rather than a confirmed bug based on the static screenshot.

## Keep

- The direct `Home · Nest · big orange next` finish pattern on `phone/04-lesson-results-home-nest-next.jpg`, `phone/11-typing-check-results.jpg`, `phone/26-tricky-keys-results.jpg` and `phone/31-word-challenge-results.jpg`. This directly fixes the owner's observed dead ends.
- The home egg finger cue that disappears once learned (`phone/01`, `phone/06`), descriptive Back labels, Nest's persistent tab and ready-to-hatch badge (`phone/28`, `phone/32`).
- The visual Tricky Keys target card at the top of Lessons, blue warm-up ring concept, and quick nonpunitive improvement/egg rewards. Adjust explanation and layout, not the core learning strategy.

**Verification needed before closure:** automated 412×740 and 360×640 screenshots verifying the last virtual key is tappable with no vertical scroll in EVERY active input mode; stress results with eight rewards; touch vs keyboard hint snapshots; class waiting/teacher start states; text scaling/VoiceOver for bottom-fixed actions. This is a design/code audit of provided screenshots, not a claim to have run the live app interactively.
