# Nest, egg and hatch polish — second opinion

**Verdict:** much stronger visual foundation; keep cel-shaded style and two-layer nest. Still a little flat/regular for the game's special reward moment. Inspected `phone-0-home.jpg` and `phone-1-nest.jpg` on the requested branch, plus `nest.ts`, `eggNest.ts`, `nest.ts` art and CSS on main. Other requested screenshot filenames were not accessible; hatch observations below are from code, not witnessed animation. No device/performance test.

1. **High — Home, phone-0-home:** the nest reads as a tiny brown saucer under an egg because the gold progress chip covers almost its entire front. Raise the stage 12–16 CSS px and move the existing chip beneath or slightly offset from it, retaining the dragon as the largest focal point. Increase nest silhouette thickness slightly at home size only; avoid covering Ashwing. No additional UI text.
2. **High — Nest, phone-1-nest:** egg and bowl have excellent clear silhouette, but horizontal twig bands look like stacked wood slats/basket rather than a natural woven nest. `game/art/nest.ts`: vary twig directions and length, add 3–4 overlapping diagonal branches, fewer perfectly parallel full-width arcs, thicker irregular ends, with dark occlusion behind front rim. Keep the rim overlap and bright egg. No texture noise or filters.
3. **Medium — Nest, phone-1-nest:** flame icon lands immediately to left of 50% at the fill boundary, so meter reads like a label jammed on a moving edge. Keep percentage stationary in center above fill with dark stroke/backplate, leave flame on one fixed side or remove it from the center. At 0/25/50/100% make text contrast independent of fill. Quarter divisions are useful; retain them.
4. **Medium — Nest, phone-1-nest:** frame and button share heavy gold/purple highlights; gold glow around the primary is wide and muddy. Tighten its halo to ~8px, darken inner alcove edge, add a small base shadow under the bowl so the artwork has depth and the egg's warm glow is focal. Retain current card silhouette and no extra gradients on animated art.
5. **Medium — Hatch mid-taps/charge/reveal, implementation only:** charging runs 560ms before opening, then shell fly animation nominally .7s, but egg button is removed 220ms after open, truncating much of that animation and nestDrop (.5s). Stage dragon behind shell with opacity/scale reveal, let fragments clear for ~450–650ms or use actual animation completion; align flash/ring and sound at burst. Keep fast reduced-motion branch; do not make the last tap feel unresponsive. Verify actual video, since no reveal screenshot was retrievable.
6. **Medium — Hatch reveal, implementation only:** 46 streak + 40 spark + 60 confetti emit at once alongside dragon art and shell animation. Prioritize readable dragon silhouette: move confetti emission later by ~200ms and make reduced-motion branch omit major particle bursts and shell/flash rather than just shortening timers (check shared particle implementation first). Keep distinctive tribe-colored glow. Profile old iPad and verify contrast/motion sensitivity; don't claim performance regression without measurement.
7. **Low — New dragon card, implementation only:** reveal settles at 2.9s then FLIP animation 650ms; keep transition if it feels smooth. On short landscape iPad check the portrait and Done button remain visible; avoid forcing full-page scroll just to leave the reward. No new controls. Preserve current frame and perch style rather than adding ornamental clutter.

Skip new features, rules, words, particle effects and extra card borders. The visible home and nest improvements are worth keeping. Before visual signoff, capture phone/iPad mid-tap, charge, full reveal and card with a rare dragon; record at 60fps to verify fragments actually complete. The current branch's later images were unavailable through connector; don't treat this as a full visual pass.
