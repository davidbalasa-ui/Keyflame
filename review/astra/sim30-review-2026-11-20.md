## Design calls

| Call | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Change | Pilot 85% on the opening lesson before changing the whole unit. Keep the later 90% rule. Offer **Try without a timer** after two misses and calculate any “keys away” cue from the actual attempt. The scripted slip rates cannot establish a teaching threshold. |
| B | Agree | Keep the early finish if it is intentional. Fade the remaining prompts and explain **Dash boost carried you across the finish** before results. Check this with real children before tuning reward or placement rules. |
| C | Agree | Use **Lessons passed**. Where there is also a count of attempts, label it **Attempts**. |
| D | Change | Make the teacher start the main class path. Show how many can join, a visible countdown, and **Join class race** on Home and in the lobby. Do not pull a rider out of an active lesson or solo race. Show the teacher how many accepted before launch. |
